## - What I Do

I focus on backend development and automation, mainly working with Node.js to build scripts, bots, and small tools that help simplify repetitive tasks.
Currently, I’m learning and exploring **Web3 and blockchain**, especially how automation can be applied to both on-chain and off-chain workflows. Most of my experience comes from hands-on practice and experimentation. I’m still early in the journey, but I stay consistent, curious, and focused on improving step by step.

**Open to:** Backend learning projects • Automation ideas • Web3 collaborations.
